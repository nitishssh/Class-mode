## 0. Reframe the scoreboard first

The bear case's decisive point is Section 5: even flawless execution = ₹24–48K MRR by December. Correct — **so revenue is the wrong 2026 scoreboard.** A solo founder's end-of-2026 win is proof of three repeatabilities, in this order:

1. **A school pays and keeps paying** (WTP is real, not polite),
2. **Teachers use it without you in the room** (adoption defies the 85% base rate),
3. **School #2 arrives cheaper than school #1** (a channel exists).

Anyone can then finance/scale that in 2027 — via SISFS (₹20L grant + ₹50L, needs exactly this evidence, per your PROGRAMS.md), revenue, or a raise when the category thaws. Without those three, no amount of 2026 hustle matters. Every play below serves one of the three.

## 1. Beat the price problem: sell recovered money, not software

The bear case is right that ₹6,000/month is a visible cost to a ₹1.2L/month school. So never sell it as a cost.

- **The audit (today) collects the number that matters:** what the school loses to unpaid/late fees and what it already spends on diaries, SMS packs, or exam printing. In a 300-student school, **if even 3% of annual fees go uncollected, that's ₹43,000/year** (300 × ₹400 × 12 × 3%) — 7 months of Class-mode. Don't assert the 3%; *measure it in their register during the audit* and price against their number.
- **Fee-season alignment:** APS cash arrives when fees arrive. Offer annual billing at fee-collection time, monthly only as fallback. A ₹60–70K annual line item negotiated in fee season beats ₹6K/month extracted in the lean months.
- **Floor discipline (bear-case failure mode #4):** the printed offer is the price. The permitted concession is time (extend the unbilled setup window), never the per-student rate. A price cut you can't reverse; a free month you can.

**Number & date:** first invoice actually paid (money moved) by **Aug 14** — the same day the bear case uses as its fail line.

## 2. Beat the adoption base rate: design for the 15%

The research says tools fail when they add steps ([SchoolAI](https://schoolai.com/blog/why-edtech-adoption-fails-in-schools), [Cambridge](https://www.cambridge.org/partnership/research/Why-EdTech-fails-without-trained-teachers)). The winning 15% subtract steps and keep a human champion. Concretely:

- **Match the paper register exactly** — the plan's design spec already binds this (status taxonomy mirrors the school's actual marks; 44px targets; vernacular). TD1 (language) gets answered today at the audit. The vernacular pass ships *before* the streak clock starts Sep 8.
- **Appoint a champion, not a user base:** one teacher, chosen with the principal, becomes the school's Class-mode person. You train the champion; the champion trains the rest. This is the only known antidote to "leadership commitment fades after rollout" — and it converts your service intensity (the bear case's ceiling) into a transferable asset.
- **The principal gets a ritual, not a login:** the daily absence summary and the term report land in the principal's hands (WhatsApp message you send manually at first — honest, labeled manual, per the approved plan). The principal's habit is what makes the teacher's habit stick. Dependence — your thesis's moat — is built at the principal's desk.
- **Instrumented, not assumed:** attendance_view/report_view events (shipped this week) make adoption measurable per teacher per day. The weekly metrics script is the adoption truth; zeros stay zeros.

**Number & date:** ≥60% of teachers marking ≥4 days/week with zero founder visits in the measurement week, by **Sep 30**.

## 3. Beat the channel problem: cluster + referral, never cold

The bear case prices founder-led cold sales at 1-in-5 and 2 schools/month. Both improve only with warmth and geography:

- **Cluster rule:** every next school within 30 minutes of school #1. One neighborhood = one support radius = one reputation pool. APS owners in a locality know each other (many belong to associations — NISA claims 400K+ member-adjacent schools nationally); a working register at one school is visible proof at the next.
- **The referral ask is part of the S4 script:** whatever today's answer, ask the principal: "which two principals should see this term report?" A referred visit converts at multiples of cold (and bear-case falsifier #3 — an unengineered referral by Oct 31 — becomes engineered-then-organic).
- **The artifact does the selling:** the printed term report (grayscale, letterhead-style, per the design spec) is a leave-behind that a principal shows other principals. Marketing budget: the cost of paper.
- **S5 (candidate list) executes inside the cluster rule** — 3–5 schools, all within the radius, intro paths via school #1's principal and the local association chapter.

**Number & date:** school #2 signed by **Oct 31**, sourced via school #1; CAC measured in founder-hours and printed pages, target <50% of school #1's.

## 4. Beat the solo ceiling: the founder stops being the product by December

- **Aug–Sep:** founder does everything, but writes the onboarding playbook as it happens (every step that worked at school #1, in order, in the school's language).
- **Oct:** school #2 is onboarded *by the playbook + champion model*, founder present but hands-off — the playbook is the product being tested.
- **Nov–Dec:** support moves to one weekly WhatsApp office-hour per school + the manual summaries automated (only after the streak evidence, per the demand-first rule).
- **Cash mechanics run in parallel, none displacing school-facing hours** (your 20% ops budget): Founders Hub ($1K Azure — kills the infra bill) this week; DPIIT after entity; **SISFS application the week after the second school signs** — a signed paid pilot plus adoption metrics is precisely the evidence that program funds, and ₹20L grant ≈ 3+ years of the bear case's infra-and-travel burn.

**Number & date:** by **Dec 31**, ≤1 founder school-day/week across all schools, measured from your own calendar.

## 5. What we deliberately do NOT do in 2026 (the bear case's traps, refused)

- **No AI as a selling point.** Goldman Sachs' skeptics are directionally right for this buyer today; your own outage data (zero complaints) proves nobody's paying for generation. Gemini billing gets linked for the day-14 precondition, capped, and AI stays a quiet Lab feature gated on payment — per the approved thesis.
- **No feature war with Teachmint.** Free-and-funded wins feature wars. We sell the register + the principal's ritual + a human in the school. Depth in one workflow, not breadth.
- **No expansion before the audit justifies it** (binding sequencing guards). No second wedge, no parent app, no marketplace. The wedge is attendance→report→fees; everything else waits for 2027.
- **No unpaid pilots.** The bear case's failure mode #1 (polite yes, never pays) is refused contractually: the offer has a signature line and billing starts at marking, not at "interest."
