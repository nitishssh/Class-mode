# S1 Call Kit — book the visit (Mon 31 Aug 2026)

_Same-day kit: the date above is the day you use it. If it is stale, that is the finding._

**Call window:** 8:30–9:30 AM (after assembly, before classes settle — the principal is findable). If no answer, retry at 10:30 and 12:30 before falling back to the WhatsApp message.

**Goal of the call:** a time on the calendar TODAY. Nothing else. No price, no pitch, no features on the phone — the offer conversation happens in person, after the audit (binding sequencing guard #1).

## The script

> Namaste ji, main Nitish bol raha hoon — Class-mode wala. Main aaj school aana chahta hoon, bas ek ghante ke liye. Mujhe dekhna hai ki aapke teachers register kaise bharte hain, aur uske baad aapse 15 minute chahiye — ek proposal hai jo main saamne rakh kar dikhana chahta hoon. Aaj kis time aana theek rahega?

(English: "I'd like to come today for one hour — to see how your teachers actually keep the register, and then 15 minutes with you; I have a proposal I want to put in front of you in person. What time today works?")

**Then be quiet.** Let them pick a time. Any time today is a win.

## Objection handling

- **"Aaj busy hai / next week"** → "Sirf ek ghanta, main aapke time pe aaunga — subah ya lunch ke baad, jo theek ho. Kal ka bhi chal jayega." (Take ANY same-week slot; today > tomorrow > never. Do not leave the call without a date and time.)
- **"Phone pe hi bata do"** → "Jo dikhana hai woh kagaz pe hai, 15 minute se zyada nahi lunga. Aamne-saamne hi theek rahega." (The offer goes on the table, not on the phone.)
- **"Kitna kharcha hai?"** → "Wahi to saamne rakh kar batana chahta hoon — 15 minute. Aap dekh kar hi decide karna." (Price only in person, printed, with the manual-vs-automated table visible.)
- **"Kaunsa app? Humne dekha nahi"** → Don't demo on the phone. "Isiliye aana chahta hoon — pehle main dekhunga aap kaise kaam karte hain, phir dikhaunga."

## If no answer by 12:30 — WhatsApp fallback

> Namaste ji, Nitish (Class-mode). Aaj school aakar 1 ghanta dena chahta hoon — teachers ka register workflow samajhne ke liye, aur aapke liye ek likha hua proposal hai. Kya aaj [3 baje] aa sakta hoon? Ek line ka reply kaafi hai. 🙏

1. ~40 min: sit with 1–2 teachers during a real attendance moment; watch, don't pitch
2. ~5 min: breathe, pick your strongest observed fact ("aapke yahan X hota hai...")
3. ~15 min: principal's office → offer on the table → ask → write down the exact words of the answer
