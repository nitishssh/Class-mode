## Short description

Attendance, parent alerts, fees and school operations in one app.

## Full description

Class Mode gives school staff and families a focused mobile view of the school day.

Teachers can open today's classes, mark attendance quickly, work through a temporary network
loss, and see whether absence alerts were queued or sent. Principals and school administrators
can review live attendance coverage and fee status without returning to a desktop dashboard.

Parents can link a child with a school-issued code, check attendance history, review fee dues and
payment history, and opt in to absence notifications on a supported physical device.

Access is role-based and tied to a Class Mode school account. The app does not display ads and
does not sell personal information. Availability of individual features depends on the school.
